# Support

## Community edition

After the public GitHub mirror is published, use [GitHub issues](https://github.com/cwirz/juraguard/issues) for reproducible bugs, documentation problems, and focused feature requests. Search existing issues first and remove credentials, tokens, private URLs, and production data from reports.

Community support is best effort. It has no response-time or resolution SLA. Maintainers may close requests outside the documented product scope.

Do not report vulnerabilities publicly. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Managed service

Managed-beta account or service questions are separate from community support. Contact [info@pyango.ch](mailto:info@pyango.ch). The current managed beta is free; final pricing and post-beta support terms have not been announced.
