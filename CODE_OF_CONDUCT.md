# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a respectful, inclusive, and practical community. Examples of welcome behavior include constructive feedback, empathy, clear technical disagreement, and respect for different experience levels and backgrounds.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [info@pyango.ch](mailto:info@pyango.ch) with `Juraguard conduct` in the subject. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect reporters' privacy where possible, and may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when needed.

Good-faith criticism of technical decisions is not harassment. Enforcement decisions focus on behavior and impact.
